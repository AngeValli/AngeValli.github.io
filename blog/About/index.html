About

I am 29 years old. I am French and Spanish. I was born and raised in Port-Saint-Louis-Du-Rhône, near Marseille, France.

Curriculum

Between mid-July and mid-August 2026, I was a visiting scholar at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, at the School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Division of Mathematical Sciences, in the research group of Prof. Ariel Neufeld. I worked with Dr. Dawen Wu on solving high-dimensional systems of ordinary differential equations using physics-informed neural networks.

I conducted my PhD under the supervision of Prof. Abdel Lisser and Prof. Sihem Tebbani at CentraleSupélec. I defended my PhD on July 2, 2026. The reporters of my PhD are Prof. Miguel Lejeune, George Washington University and Prof. Mounir Haddou, INSA Rennes. The video recording of my presentation during my PhD thesis defense is available here. The president of the jury was Antoine Girard, Senior Researcher at CNRS and the other jury members were Prof. Francesca Maggioni, Università degli Studi di Bergamo and Prof. Jia Liu, Xi'an Jiaotong University. The follow-up committee for my PhD thesis consisted of Prof. Janny M. Y. Leung, University of Macau and Prof. Véronique Vèque, CentraleSupélec. More details about my PhD are available here, including the defense and pre-defense reports of my thesis. Papers written and talks given during my PhD are presented here. Professional services given during my PhD are presented here. I worked as a teaching assistant at Université Paris-Saclay for academic years 2023/2024 and 2024/2025. Materials can be found here.

I worked as a quantitative analyst contractor at BNP Paribas CIB, within the quantitative research department, focusing on equity derivatives. I hold a master’s degree in Mathematics for Finance and Data (formerly DEA Lamberton), jointly offered by Université Gustave Eiffel and Université Paris-Est Créteil, and co-accredited with École nationale des ponts et chaussées, obtained in 2022. This program included an internship at Next Gate Tech, a fintech company based in Luxembourg, where I worked as a quantitative developer. I also hold a master’s degree in artificial intelligence from ENSTA, obtained in 2021, during which I completed an internship at Generali France where I worked as a data scientist.
